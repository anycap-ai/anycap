## What's Changed

### Audio Model Positioning

- **Doubao Seed Audio 1.0 is now described as speech-first audio generation.** Model discovery calls out speech synthesis, multi-speaker dialogue, and complete audio scenes generated from text, audio, or image references.
- **Audio guidance now matches the broader voice workflow.** Embedded CLI examples lead with spoken introductions and reference-guided voice or image scenes while retaining background music, ambience, and sound effects as complete-scene elements.
- **Existing commands remain compatible.** Continue using `anycap audio models` and `anycap audio generate` with `text-to-audio`, `audio-to-audio`, or `image-to-audio`; this release does not change request or response shapes.
